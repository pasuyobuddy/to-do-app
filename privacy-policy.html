To-Do Alarm – Privacy Policy

App by PasuyoLabs (Antonio De Guzman) · Effective date: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what information the To-Do Alarm app ("the app") uses, and why. We keep it simple: your tasks stay on your phone.

1. Your tasks stay on your device

The tasks you create (title, date, time and alarm setting) are saved only on your own device. We do not have a server, we do not upload your tasks anywhere, and we cannot see them. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your tasks are deleted.

The app has no sign-up and no account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

2. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications – to show the alarm when a task is due.
  • Exact alarms – so the alarm rings at the exact minute you chose, even when the app is closed.
  • Vibration – so the alarm can vibrate.
  • Start at boot – so your alarms still ring after you restart your phone.
  • Internet – only to load ads (see below).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (for example phone model, Android version, language),
  • your approximate location based on IP address,
  • how you interact with ads (for example whether an ad was shown or tapped).

This information is collected and handled by Google, not by us. You can read how Google uses it here: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent message so you can choose how your data is used for ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your information. Apart from the ad information collected by Google as described above, the app does not share any data with anyone.

6. Security

Because your tasks never leave your device, their safety depends on your phone's own security, such as your screen lock.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
